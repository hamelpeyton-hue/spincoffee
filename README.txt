THE SPIN — WEBSITE FILES

Open index.html to view the website. Keep the assets folder, styles.css, script.js, and thank-you.html beside index.html.

Files:
index.html — homepage and contact form
styles.css — colors, typography, layout, and mobile styles
script.js — menu prices, category tabs, and mobile navigation
thank-you.html — contact form confirmation
assets/ — supplied logo and photos

Edit drinks and prices in the classics and specials arrays in script.js.
Edit site text in index.html. Colors are at the top of styles.css.

DEPLOY TO YOUR EXISTING NETLIFY SITE
Drag this folder's contents into the manual deployment area for the existing spincoffee site. Upload index.html at the top level, not inside a second folder. This package has no build step or dependencies.

CONTACT FORM
The form uses Netlify Forms. Enable form detection in your Netlify site's Forms settings, then deploy. Test a submission after deployment and configure notification emails in Netlify. The form will not send from a file opened locally or on GitHub Pages. No mailbox has been connected or tested here.

Fonts load from Google Fonts; a serif and Arial fallback keep the page usable offline. Photos load from the included assets folder.

Content and prices were retained from the supplied website and should be reviewed before publishing. No event dates, address, opening hours, or business history were independently verified. Replace the editorial story text if the business has approved wording.
